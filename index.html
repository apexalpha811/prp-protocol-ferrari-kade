The Optimized Protocol (Amable et al., 2013)

Step g-force Time Temp Brake
01 Spin 1 (Soft) 300 g 5 min RT Off
→ Discard RBC layer, keep plasma + buffy coat
02 Spin 2 (Hard) 700 g 17 min RT Off
→ Discard PPP (top), keep platelet pellet, resuspend in ~1 mL residual plasma
RESULT: ~1,288 × 10³ platelets/µL

Key Variables That Matter

01. Brake Off

Using the centrifuge brake on the first spin disrupts the buffy coat layer and reduces yield. Always use brake-off or lowest brake setting.

02. Rotor Type

Horizontal/swing-bucket rotors give cleaner separation than fixed-angle rotors. Fixed-angle rotors pack platelets against the tube wall.

03. Temperature

Room temperature (20-24°C) is standard. Cold temperatures can activate platelets prematurely.

04. Tube Type

Glass tubes activate platelets. Use plastic (polypropylene or PET) tubes with anticoagulant (ACD-A or sodium citrate).

Technical Specifications

The Verdict

Maximum platelets/mL is achieved with 300 g × 5 min, then 700 g × 17 min, no brake, room temperature, in plastic tubes with ACD-A. Expect ~5-6× the whole blood platelet concentration.
